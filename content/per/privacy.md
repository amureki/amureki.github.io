**Effective date: July 28, 2026**

This privacy policy applies only to the Per iOS app.

## Data stored by Per

Per stores the information you enter—including subscription names, prices, billing-cycle details, usage records, and app preferences—locally on your device. The app uses this information only to provide its subscription usage tracking, history, and cost-per-use features.

## Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect personal data or usage data from Per. The app does not transmit that data off your device for developer or third-party access.

Per has no accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Per does not sell or share data with third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual subscriptions within Per.

If Per migrates data from an older app version, it retains the original on-device store as a recovery copy to prevent data loss. To remove all data stored by Per on this device, including recovery data and preferences, delete the app. Offloading the app may preserve its data.

Because the developer does not collect or store your data, there is no server-held data to access, delete, or withdraw consent for.

## Security

Per relies on the security protections provided by iOS and your device. The app does not transmit your data over a network.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if Per's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

For privacy questions, email [per@amureki.me](mailto:per@amureki.me).
