**Effective date: September 25, 2026**

This privacy policy applies only to the Per iOS app.

## Data stored by Per

Per stores item names, prices, currencies, billing details, usage records, and preferences locally on your device. It uses this information to provide item tracking, usage history, and cost-per-use calculations.

## Data collection and sharing

The developer does not receive your item or usage records. Per has no user accounts, app-level cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics SDK, or tracking. Per does not sell your data.

Device backups, including iCloud Backup if enabled, may contain local app data under your device and Apple Account settings.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores of Per Plus through the App Store. Per uses StoreKit transaction information to verify whether Plus is available, including changes after refunds or revocation. Purchase verification can contact Apple. Per does not receive your payment card details or send your item records to Apple as part of a purchase.

Apple's handling of purchase information is described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer may receive App Store sales and financial reports provided by Apple.

## Support

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the information you choose to include and uses it to respond. Do not include information that is not needed for your request.

Opening legal links uses Safari or your selected browser. Those websites have their own data handling practices.

## Data retention and deletion

You can delete individual items in Per. The app may retain original local stores from previous versions as recovery copies to prevent data loss. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data and preferences from the device. Manage any device backups separately.

Deleting Per does not refund the purchase or delete Apple's transaction records. You can restore Plus using the Apple Account that purchased it. Contact Apple for purchase and refund requests.

## Security

Per relies on iOS device security and Apple's StoreKit purchase protections. The developer does not operate a server that stores your item or usage history.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if Per's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, email [per@amureki.me](mailto:per@amureki.me).
