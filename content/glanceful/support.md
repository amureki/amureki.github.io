For help with Glanceful, email [hi@amureki.me](mailto:hi@amureki.me?subject=Glanceful%20Support). Include your iOS version and a short description of the problem.

## Add the widget

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, select the Lock Screen, then tap **Add Widgets**. Choose Glanceful and add the inline or rectangular widget.

## Rotation timing

iOS controls exact widget refresh timing. Hourly or daily changes may appear shortly after the requested time.

## Protect private text

Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can read text shown by the widget. Do not display sensitive information there.

For details about local storage and data collection, read the [privacy policy](@/glanceful/privacy.md).
